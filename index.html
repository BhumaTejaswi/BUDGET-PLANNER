ABOUT BUDGET PLANNER

A simpler way to understand your money.

Budget Planner is a web-based application designed to make personal budget management simple and accessible.

The platform provides users with a structured way to track their income and expenses, manage budgets, set financial goals and understand their spending.

It also provides an administrative workspace for managing and monitoring the platform.
